# Frozen Results and Interpretation

These derived outputs were generated by project version `3.0.0-risk-controlled`. The full configuration and data provenance are recorded in `../results/run_metadata.txt`.

## Headline finding

From the common comparison start of 2019-08-01 through 2026-07-30, the risk-controlled portfolio returned 11.02% annualized with 15.67% volatility, a -28.99% maximum drawdown, and a 0.75 Sharpe ratio. The fixed classic 20-day / top-10% momentum comparator returned 18.23% annualized with 37.09% volatility, a -59.23% drawdown, and a 0.64 Sharpe ratio.

The risk-controlled design therefore had a 30.24-percentage-point smaller historical drawdown than the classic comparator, but gave up 7.21 percentage points of annualized return. Same-universe equal-weight buy-and-hold earned more (19.93% annualized) and had a higher Sharpe ratio (0.84). This is evidence of a risk-return trade-off, not proof of superiority or optimality.

## Files

- `../results/analysis_report.html`: self-contained research narrative; keep beside its two PNG files.
- `../results/equity_curve.csv`: daily return, NAV, cost, turnover, exposure, and comparator series.
- `../results/performance_metrics.csv`: return, risk, drawdown, and tail metrics.
- `../results/risk_control_log.csv`: signal-date risk inputs, target exposure, and executed exposure.
- `../results/integrity_checks.csv`: 10 internal identities, all passing in the frozen run.
- `../results/temporal_holdout_diagnostic.csv`: development/holdout split, explicitly retrospective.
- `../results/subperiod_performance.csv`: full, first-half, and second-half performance.
- `../results/parameter_sensitivity.csv`: pure-momentum signal sensitivity, not an optimized risk overlay.
- `../results/rebalance_log.csv`: rankings and target-weight stages for audit.
- `../results/data_quality.csv`: quote coverage by stock.
- `../results/run_metadata.txt`: source, versions, parameters, assumptions, and effective dates.

The temporal split does not establish genuine out-of-sample performance. Full downloaded observations, local caches, and daily position matrices are excluded to reduce redistribution and repository size.
